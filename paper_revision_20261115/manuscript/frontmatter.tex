\begin{abstract}
We present \SHARP, an ecliptic survey for asteroid astrometry and
photometry with the Xinglong 60/90~cm Schmidt telescope. Its nominal
$2.94^\circ\times2.95^\circ$ field of view, 30~s white-light exposures,
and repeated visits within a night provide positions and brightness
measurements for known asteroids and enable searches for moving sources.
During its first year, from 2025 November~15 to 2026 November~15, the
survey obtained \yrNexp\ science exposures on \yrNnight\ observing nights.
The observations cover $\yrArea~\degsq$, including
$\yrAreaThree~\degsq$ observed at least three times within one night.
The median interval between successive visits to a field is
\yrCadence~min. The median catalog $5\sigma$ model depth is
\yrDepth~mag on the Gaia-calibrated white-light scale. We associate
\yrNAssoc\ measurements with \yrNKnown\ cataloged asteroids within
$1\asec$ of their predicted positions; their median radial
observed-minus-computed separation is $\yrOC\asec$.
Among known asteroids detected at least three times in a night within
the search's angular-rate range, \yrRetention\% are recovered as
single-night linkages. Image inspection retains \yrNCandidates\
candidate linkages from the independent motion search.
These observations establish SHARP's first-year coverage, temporal
sampling, and measurement quality, and provide a basis for asteroid
time-series studies and follow-up of selected moving sources.
\end{abstract}
